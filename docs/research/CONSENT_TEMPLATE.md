# JANUS Phase 2A consent template

> **TEMPLATE — REQUIRES HUMAN, LEGAL, PRIVACY, AND ETHICS REVIEW.**
> This document is not approval, legal advice, or a ready-to-use consent form.

Before real collection, an authorized reviewer must replace every
`NEEDS_DECISION` item and approve a versioned final document.

## Study information

- Research owner/contact: `NEEDS_DECISION`
- Purpose: study whether response patterns differ between groups under
  ambiguous, preference-based challenges.
- What participants do: choose among options in approximately
  `NEEDS_DECISION` challenges/minutes.
- Data collected: pseudonymous ID, choices, option order, challenge sequence,
  bucketed response latency, correction count, coarse input modality, completion
  or withdrawal status.
- Data not required: name, email, precise location, persistent device/browser
  fingerprint, raw pointer trajectory, biometric data.
- Risks/discomforts: `NEEDS_REVIEW`
- Benefits/compensation: `NEEDS_DECISION`
- Voluntary participation and withdrawal process: `NEEDS_REVIEW`
- Retention/deletion policy: `NEEDS_DECISION`
- Who may access data: `NEEDS_DECISION`
- Publication/data-sharing policy: `NEEDS_DECISION`
- Jurisdiction, minimum age, and complaint channel: `NEEDS_DECISION`

## Consent action

The participant must actively confirm the reviewed statement before any
challenge response is accepted. Declining or withdrawing must not be represented
as suspicious behavior.
